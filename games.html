Gaming Corner

I’ve played games since before I could read. In fact, I learned to read because of — and for — my DS Lite.

My parents, immigrants who didn’t grow up with technology, didn’t really understand gaming. Still, they saved up to buy me consoles anyway. To them, it was a way for me to stay connected with pop culture and engage my brain.

It’s something that’s been with me for as long as I can remember.
