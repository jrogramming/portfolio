Marketing Marketplace

In 2021, I started a fandom artist collaboration — about thirty people — on a whim and a literal coin flip because I wanted to see content that didn’t exist yet. That one decision set off a chain reaction that shaped my life in ways I could have never expected.

The next project inspired by my own went on to earn around $14k and even caught the attention of the game’s publisher.

Seeing how a community-driven idea could grow, inspire others, and reach the people behind the game itself pushed me into years of social media and entertainment e-commerce work. Marketing became the place where I could blend creativity, data, and community in a way that genuinely impacts people.

And it all started because I simply wanted to see something cool happen.
